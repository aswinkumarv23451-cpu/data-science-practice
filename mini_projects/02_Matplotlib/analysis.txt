1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
------------------------------------------------------------

This project analyzes the World Happiness Report 2024 dataset
to understand global happiness patterns and explore factors
associated with national happiness.

Python, NumPy, and Matplotlib are used to perform statistical
analysis, identify trends, detect outliers, and create
meaningful visualizations.


2. DATASET SUMMARY
------------------------------------------------------------

Dataset              : World Happiness Report 2024
Original Records     : 143
Clean Records        : 140
Incomplete Records   : 3
Total Features       : 12

Features Analyzed:
- Happiness Score
- Log GDP per Capita
- Social Support
- Healthy Life Expectancy
- Freedom to Make Life Choices
- Generosity
- Perceptions of Corruption


3. HAPPINESS STATISTICAL ANALYSIS
------------------------------------------------------------

Mean Happiness Score       : 5.5308
Median Happiness Score     : 5.8000
Standard Deviation         : 1.1770
Maximum Happiness Score    : 7.741
Minimum Happiness Score    : 1.721
Happiness Score Range      : 6.020


4. TOP AND BOTTOM COUNTRIES
------------------------------------------------------------

Happiest Country:
Finland - 7.741

Least Happy Country:
Afghanistan - 1.721

Other countries in the Top 10:
Denmark, Iceland, Sweden, Israel, Netherlands, Norway,
Luxembourg, Switzerland, and Australia.


5. REGIONAL HAPPINESS ANALYSIS
------------------------------------------------------------

Highest Regional Average:
North America and ANZ - 6.93

Second Highest Regional Average:
Western Europe - 6.84

Lowest Regional Average:
South Asia - 3.90

Sub-Saharan Africa also recorded a relatively low average
happiness score of 4.33.


6. OUTLIER ANALYSIS
------------------------------------------------------------

First Quartile (Q1)          : 4.632
Median                       : 5.800
Third Quartile (Q3)          : 6.426
Interquartile Range (IQR)    : 1.795

Lower Bound                  : 1.940
Upper Bound                  : 7.324

Number of Outliers           : 6

High Happiness Outliers:
Finland, Denmark, Iceland, Sweden, and Israel.

Low Happiness Outlier:
Afghanistan.

The IQR method was used to identify values outside the
calculated lower and upper bounds.


7. CORRELATION ANALYSIS
------------------------------------------------------------

Correlation with Happiness Score:

Social Support              : 0.814
Log GDP per Capita          : 0.769
Healthy Life Expectancy     : 0.760
Freedom                     : 0.644
Perceptions of Corruption   : 0.452
Generosity                  : 0.130

Social support showed the strongest positive correlation
with happiness among the analyzed factors.

Generosity showed the weakest positive correlation.


8. KEY FINDINGS
------------------------------------------------------------

1. Finland recorded the highest happiness score, while
   Afghanistan recorded the lowest.

2. Social support, GDP per capita, and healthy life
   expectancy showed strong positive correlations with
   happiness.

3. North America and ANZ recorded the highest regional
   average happiness score.

4. South Asia recorded the lowest regional average.

5. Six countries were identified as statistical outliers
   using the IQR method.

6. Happiness levels vary considerably across countries
   and geographical regions.


9. CONCLUSION
------------------------------------------------------------

The analysis demonstrates how data science techniques can
be applied to explore global happiness patterns and identify
factors associated with quality of life.

Social support, economic conditions, and healthy life
expectancy showed particularly strong relationships with
happiness scores.

These findings represent statistical associations and do
not establish cause-and-effect relationships.

This project provided practical experience in data cleaning,
NumPy-based statistical analysis, correlation analysis,
outlier detection, and professional data visualization.
