BIKE SHARING DEMAND ANALYSIS

Business Analysis Report

Author: ASWIN KUMAR V
Technologies: Python, NumPy, Matplotlib
Dataset: UCI Bike Sharing Dataset (2011–2012)

---

1. Project Overview

This project analyses real-world bike-sharing data to understand rental demand patterns, customer behaviour, seasonal variations, hourly trends and relationships between weather conditions and rental activity.

The analysis was performed using Python and NumPy.

2. Dataset Information

- Daily records : 731
- Hourly records: 17,379
- Period        : 2011–2012
- Total rentals : 3,292,679

3. Daily Rental Demand Analysis

Daily rental demand was classified into three categories using custom thresholds.

Insight: High-demand days represented the largest category in the dataset.

13. Business Recommendations

1. Peak-hour planning: Improve bike availability during high-demand hours, especially around 5 PM.
2. Seasonal planning: Prepare additional operational capacity during fall.
3. Customer engagement: Introduce targeted offers to increase casual customer participation.
4. Weather-aware operations: Consider weather conditions when planning fleet availability.
5. Customer retention: Focus on maintaining the registered customer base.

14. Conclusion

The analysis identified important patterns in bike-sharing demand across seasons, hours, customer segments, working days and weather conditions.

NumPy was used for numerical processing, statistical calculations, grouping, sorting and correlation analysis. Matplotlib was used to create visualizations that communicate the findings.

This project demonstrates practical application of Python and NumPy to real-world data analysis and business insight generation.
